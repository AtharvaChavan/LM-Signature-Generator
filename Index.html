How to add in gmail-Email Signature

  1. Click Copy Signature above (not the raw HTML — that button copies the actual formatted, clickable version).
  2. In Gmail, go to Settings ⚙ → See all settings → General tab.
  3. Scroll to Signature, click Create new (or select your existing one).
  4. Click inside the empty signature box, then Paste (Ctrl/Cmd + V).
  5. Scroll down and click Save Changes.
